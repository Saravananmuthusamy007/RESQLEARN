# ResqLearn Clinical Emergency Guidelines

This document outlines the standard medical protocols simulated within the ResqLearn emergency training engine.

---

## Level 1 — CPR / AED (Adult Basic Life Support)
1. **Scene Safety & Responsiveness**: Tap shoulders firmly and speak loudly ("Are you okay?").
2. **Emergency Activation**: Dispatch EMS / 911 immediately and retrieve an Automated External Defibrillator (AED).
3. **Pulse & Respiration**: Check carotid groove and observe chest rise for at least 5 seconds, but **no more than 10 seconds**.
4. **Hand Position**: Place the heel of one hand on the lower half of the sternum (center of chest), interlocking fingers over top. Avoid compressing the xiphoid process.
5. **30 Compressions**:
   - **Rate**: 100 to 120 compressions per minute.
   - **Depth**: 5.0 to 6.0 cm (2.0 to 2.4 inches).
   - **Recoil**: 100% complete chest rebound between compressions.
6. **2 Rescue Breaths**: Head-tilt / chin-lift maneuver, deliver 2 gentle breaths (1 sec each, observing chest rise).

---

## Level 2 — Severe Hemorrhage Control & Tourniquet
1. **PPE**: Don nitrile gloves before touching blood to prevent pathogen exposure.
2. **Direct Manual Pressure**: Apply 40 to 60 Newtons of continuous downward force using sterile gauze within 15 seconds.
3. **Wound Packing**: In junctional or deep wounds, tightly pack hemostatic gauze down to the bleeding vessel at the bone base.
4. **CAT Tourniquet**: Position 5 to 7 cm (2–3 inches) proximal to the injury. **NEVER apply directly over a joint**.
5. **Windlass Tightening**: Rotate windlass rod until arterial spurting stops and the distal pulse is extinguished.
6. **Timestamp**: Secure windlass in clip and write the application time (e.g. "T = 14:32") on the time strap.

---

## Level 3 — Thermal Burn Stabilization & Cooling
1. **Scene Safety**: Extinguish flames or separate victim from the thermal source immediately.
2. **Controlled Water Cooling**: Irrigate continuously with cool running water at **15°C to 20°C for 10 to 20 minutes**.
3. **Jewelry Removal**: Slide off rings, watches, and tight bracelets before tissue edema causes vascular strangulation.
4. **Avoid Dangerous Remedies**:
   - **NO ICE**: Severe vasoconstriction causes frostbite and deepens the burn necrosis.
   - **NO BUTTER / OILS**: Traps heat and leads to severe wound infections.
   - **NO BLISTER POPPING**: Intact blisters provide a sterile biological barrier.
5. **Dressing**: Loosely drape clean plastic wrap or dry sterile burn sheets over the wound without compression.
6. **Normothermia**: Cover unburned areas with a blanket to prevent hypothermia.

---

## Level 4 — Choking (Foreign Body Airway Obstruction)
1. **Severity Triage**:
   - **Mild Obstruction**: Forceful coughing and speaking $\rightarrow$ Encourage coughing; do not intervene.
   - **Severe Obstruction**: Silent cough, unable to speak or breathe, cyanosis $\rightarrow$ Immediate physical action.
2. **Positioning**: Stand to the side/behind, support the chest, and lean the victim well forward so gravity expels the object.
3. **5 Back Blows**: Deliver 5 sharp heel-of-hand blows between the shoulder blades.
4. **5 Abdominal Thrusts (Heimlich)**: Place fist above navel (below xiphoid), pull sharply inward and upward.
5. **Cadence**: Alternate 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts.
6. **Unconscious Pivot**: If victim collapses, ease to floor, call 911, and begin modified CPR immediately. Look in mouth before breaths; **NEVER perform blind finger sweeps**.

---

## Level 5 — Musculoskeletal Fracture Splinting
1. **Support**: Stabilize the injured limb manually in the position found without attempting forced realignment.
2. **Pre-Splint PMS**: Assess distal **Pulse** (radial/pedal), **Motor** (finger/toe wiggle), and **Sensory** (light touch).
3. **Splint Selection**: Pad rigid surfaces, especially over bony prominences.
4. **Two-Joint Rule**: Immobilize both the **joint above** and the **joint below** the fracture site.
5. **Safe Tension**: Secure straps at 50% to 75% tension. Avoid excessive tightness that occludes arterial circulation.
6. **Post-Splint PMS**: Recheck Pulse, Motor, and Sensory immediately. If pulse is lost, loosen straps at once!
